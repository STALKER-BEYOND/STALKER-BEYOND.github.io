Common Sense

Rules & Guidelines

While this list covers a large portion of what we'd like to uphold, it can't account for every possibility. We ask that you use common sense while you're here; just because there's no rule explicitly against it doesn't mean it's okay. If you’re unsure, contact an admin. The staff have the right to ban a player for any reason.

01Community

  1. 1.1

    You must be at least 18 years old to be whitelisted.

  2. 1.2

    No alt accounts are allowed on the Discord or the server.

  3. 1.3

    Breaking a rule in response to an actual or perceived rulebreak is never justified. Please make a report.

  4. 1.4

    Don’t help others break, or get away with breaking, the rules. You may be permanently banned for it.

02Discord

  1. 2.1

    Don’t display or post NSFW content.

  2. 2.2

    Don’t reveal the personal info of others without their consent.

  3. 2.3

    Don't share links to other communities or servers. Only self-promote in #media or #off-topic-media.

  4. 2.4

    Don’t bait, impersonate, harass, or insult people. This includes using generally offensive words or slurs.

  5. 2.5

    Try to keep posts relevant to the channel. Posts in the wrong channel may be deleted without warning.

  6. 2.6

    Soundboards and voice changers should be used in moderation. If you’re asked to stop, please stop.

  7. 2.7

    Tickets involving bug reports, compensation, or rulebreaks always require image or video proof.

  8. 2.8

    AI may not be used for applications. If you have a disability, get help from a friend or the staff.

03Gameplay

  1. 3.1

    Don’t use cheats or exploits. You may be permanently banned depending on the severity.

  2. 3.2

    If you crash or are disconnected during RP, you should try to log back in ASAP. If you can’t, you should inform the people involved through Discord or DMs.

  3. 3.3

    Don’t treat doors like they’re impenetrable. Cheesing doors for an advantage in combat or disrupting RP is prohibited. This includes any locked structure be it a faction or player base.

04Roleplay

  1. 4.1

    Erotic roleplay (ERP) of any kind is strictly not allowed and may result in a permanent ban.

  2. 4.2

    Metagaming (using IC information gained OOC to gain any sort of advantage) is not allowed.

  3. 4.3

    You should always remain IC with your actions and voice. You may only communicate OOC through text when there’s an OOC emergency, it’s required to explain a situation, or helping someone with keybinds.

  4. 4.4

    When in a radio frequency on Discord, you must have a radio in-game with a charged battery. You also must be able to ICly use your radio. If you die, you must deafen and wait 3 minutes, then leave the channel. You may rejoin once the RP is done.

  5. 4.5

    You must value the health and life of your character. Failing to do this may result in a negligence PK. Examples of what not to do include failing to comply/surrender when outgunned/outnumbered, and not fleeing from an unwinnable fight. This includes wearing the gear or patches of factions other than your own.

  6. 4.6

    Injury and torture RP are routes of RP that provide IC consequences less severe than a PK. Players who want to injury RP must ask for, or consent to, the injury before it is RP’d. Consent can be asked for or given either through DMs, a ticket, or an OOC text message in-game. Repeatedly and unreasonably declining potential injury RP is distasteful, and may be punished if reported.

  7. 4.7

    If a player dies without a PK, everyone involved is required to follow the new life rule (NLR). They must act as if the killed character were actually someone else, and the one killed must forget the events leading up to their death. They can’t return for 30 minutes, or as long as the RP is happening, whichever is longer. They also can’t loot their own body, but can receive the items from someone else once the timer is up.

  8. 4.8

    IC photos and videos may only be taken with the camera item. Any photos or videos that lack the camera’s overlay shouldn’t be taken in-character - doing so is considered metagaming.

05Combat

  1. 5.1

    Patches are required to be worn in the armband slot at all times. The only exception is when wearing a faction’s armband as a Loner. You can kill on identification (KOID) someone wearing the armband or patch of a faction hostile to your own, but you are encouraged to initiate or provide some kind of warning shot.

    A chart displaying the current relations between factions can be found at the bottom of this section.

  2. 5.2

    You can not log out to avoid death or during combat - it will be considered abuse of game mechanics. If there is an emergency, you should inform the staff via ticket. In the event of a restart, you must rejoin and wait 3-5 minutes before re-engaging in combat.

  3. 5.3

    “Pips” refer to the volume levels of voice chat. 0-pipping (not using it at all) is not allowed and falls under metagaming. In combat, 1-pipping is allowed when it would be negligent not to, such as flanking, sneaking or hiding from enemies. Aside from those circumstances, you must be 2 or 3-pipping with voice.

  4. 5.4

    You can not KOID in hubs such as the Rookie Village, 100 Rads Bar, and the Ecologist FOB in Yantar. You may still initiate in these areas, however you must have a believable and solid IC reason. You also can’t KOID outside the hub from within the hub, and vice-versa. Parties KOID’d this way should still defend themselves.

  5. 5.5

    To commence hostility with a non-KOID faction, you must initiate. A proper initiation must have a clear and verbalized demand and threat. Threats may also be conveyed through hostile actions, such as aiming a weapon, racking a bolt, etc., but is only a threat once the demand and consequence have been made and the initiation is clear. Body blocking is also considered an initiation if a verbal demand or threat is included. Attempting to bind someone is a form of initiation that doesn’t need verbalization for the victim to react. Butt-stocking and punching are considered attacks and need initiation beforehand.

  6. 5.6

    Assisting in an initiation in any way, such as covering or helping the initiator, counts as being an initiator. Anyone who wishes to not be involved should leave the area once the situation shows signs of hostility. Failing to leave while not wanting to be involved may be considered negligent.

  7. 5.7

    As a victim during an initiation, you and anyone part of your group gain the right to attack those who have initiated on you. Conversely, as the initiating party, you may attack noncompliant people, their allies, or anyone associated with the initiated party within the context of the situation at hand.

  8. 5.8

    If both parties leave an initiation without hostility, the right to attack from the initiation is revoked. However, for the next 30 minutes either party may reinitiate for the same reason.

  9. 5.9

    The responsibility of confirming initiation or KOID on a target falls on the attacker. If a player mis-IDs a third party as a target they may be held accountable for RDM. This also applies to the target, with exception to the third party being negligent, like wearing gear or patches of a faction that the target is able to KOID.

06Robbery

When robbing someone, keep in mind that they put time and effort into the equipment you want to take. Try not to steal everything they have, and you should leave or replace anything essential to their survival such as clothes, gas mask, gun, ammo and meds. Initiating a robbery using spawn equipment is considered negligent, and may result in a negligence PK. Whether a robbery is fair or negligent is determined by staff on a case-by-case basis, so if you think you've been robbed unfairly or negligently please make a report.

07Voice Identification

Identifying a character based on their voice is only allowed when there is another piece of evidence that can be used to associate the voice to the character, such as the context of the encounter, their equipment, mentions of their name or something only they would know. If you’re unsure, please contact an admin.

08Permanent Kills

Permanent kills or PKs are a server mechanic that is used to prevent a character arc or storyline from stagnating, and provide a realistic and tangible consequence for situations that would normally result in a player respawning and repeating the actions that got them killed. When someone receives approval to PK another character, it goes both ways - if they are killed by the target in their attempt, they will be PK’d.

PKs should always be done in good faith, so try to give the target a chance to roleplay before being killed.

  1. 8.1

    Any cheating or metagaming used to carry out a PK immediately invalidates and reverses the PK.

    The PKing party may also be held responsible for rulebreak.

  2. 8.2

    The existence or reason of the PK approval should not be revealed OOC to the target until after the PK has taken place. If either are revealed, this may result in metagame and will be handled accordingly.

  3. 8.3

    The party with the approved PK must initiate on the target, and are the only ones allowed to carry out the PK. The initiator(s) must mention why they are killing the target before doing so. Once the initiation has started, the target dying in the presence of the initiator(s) will result in a PK. Anyone with the target is able to be killed by the initiator(s), but should be held to NLR.

  4. 8.4

    The execution of a PK needs to be recorded and readily available for review by staff. If a PK is done without a recording, it may be invalidated or reversed.

PK Approval

In order for a PK to be approved, some specific needs must be met:

  • Is a PK the most realistic and reasonable solution to resolve the problem?
  • Is the act of killing this character what your character would do realistically?
  • Has the target of the PK done something IC that would warrant being permanently killed?
  • Is the act of PKing this character proportionate to the damage or negative consequences their actions have caused?

Lacking even just one of those factors may result in the PK being refused. PKs must be proactive, meaning the ticket has to be approved before the execution of the PK. Any PKs that are not approved beforehand (excluding negligence PKs) are considered invalid and will be reversed. Negligence PKs are reactive, meaning the ticket can be made after the death. Players may self-PK at any time for any reason.
