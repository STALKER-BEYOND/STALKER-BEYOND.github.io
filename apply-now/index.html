APPLICATIONS

Before You Apply

Whitelist & Factions

Every player needs an approved whitelist application before joining the server. You must be 18 or older to apply. Read the Rules and the Lore in full first, and answer every question in your own words. Applications written with AI or copied from another source will be denied.

How to Apply

  1. 01Read the Rules & Lore
  2. 02Submit Your Application
  3. 03Open a Ticket on Discord

Once you've submitted an application, open a ticket in our Discord to let staff know it's ready for review.
